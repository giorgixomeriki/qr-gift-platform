# QR Starr: Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: [[date of approval]]

## What you buy and when it's delivered

The activation fee makes your digital greeting available on a QR Starr card.
Delivery is **immediate**: the greeting goes live on the card as soon as Bank
of Georgia confirms your payment. The amount is shown in GEL before you pay.

## Before payment

You can stop at any point before paying. Nothing is charged until you
complete payment on Bank of Georgia's page, and an unpaid greeting is never
shown to anyone.

## After activation

Because the greeting is delivered immediately at your request, the right to
withdraw ends once it is activated [[LEGAL: confirm with the Law of Georgia on
the Protection of Consumer Rights; requires the pre-payment consent step,
README D3]]. Your statutory rights are not affected.

## When you get your money back

You will get a **full refund** to the card you paid with if:

1. **you were charged but the greeting was not activated** (for example, the
   card had already been used, or the payment arrived after the attempt
   failed). This is refunded automatically;
2. **you were charged more than once** for the same greeting. The extra charge
   is refunded automatically;
3. **a fault on our side** stops an activated greeting from opening, and we
   can't fix it within [[N days]];
4. **the payment wasn't made by you** (please also contact your bank);
5. [[DECISION D4: any goodwill case, e.g. a request within N hours of
   activation if the greeting has not been opened yet?]]

## How to ask

Email [[support email]] with the card's code (printed under the QR) and the
date of payment. We reply within [[N]] working days.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment card through Bank of Georgia. We
always refund the full activation fee. Once the bank confirms, it typically
reaches your card within [[N working days — confirm with BOG (C4)]], depending
on your card issuer. A refunded greeting may be taken offline
[[DECISION: or stays available?]].

## Card disputes (chargebacks)

If you dispute a payment with your bank, please contact us first; we can
usually resolve it faster. If a chargeback is made, the bank's decision
applies and we may contest it with evidence of delivery (activation and
opening times, never the greeting's content).

## The physical card

The card itself is provided by the partner shop. Questions about it go to the
shop, under its own terms.
