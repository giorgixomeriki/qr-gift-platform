## 1. The service

1. A QR Starr card is a physical card with a QR code. You receive it from one
   of our partner shops. The card itself is provided by that shop on its own
   terms. Questions about buying the physical card go to the shop.
2. By scanning the card you can create a **digital greeting**: a written
   message, up to three photos, one video and one voice recording, in a design
   you choose. You can preview it exactly as the recipient will see it.
3. To make the greeting available on the card you pay a one-time **activation
   fee**. The amount in Georgian lari (GEL) is shown on the checkout screen
   before you pay. There is no subscription or other charge.
4. The greeting is delivered **immediately** after successful payment: it
   becomes available on that card at once, and the recipient opens it by
   scanning the same card.

## 2. Important: the card is the key

5. **Anyone who holds the activated card, or has its link, can open the
   greeting.** There is no password or PIN. Give the card only to the person it
   is meant for, and do not share photos of its QR code.
6. **After activation the greeting can't be changed.** Check the preview
   carefully before paying.
7. Your editing access before activation is kept on the device you used to
   create the greeting (see the Privacy Policy, cookies). If you change device
   or clear your browser data before paying, you may have to start again.
