1.The service
A QR Starr card is a physical card with a QR code, which you receive from one of our partner shops.
By scanning an unused card you can create a digital greeting: a written message, up to 3 photos, one video and one voice note, in a design you choose.
To make the greeting available on the card you pay a one-time activation fee, shown in Georgian lari (GEL) before you pay.
The greeting is delivered digitally and immediately: it becomes available on the card as soon as payment is confirmed, and the recipient opens it by scanning the same card.

What a greeting can hold, and its limits, are described on How it works.

2.Who can use the service

You must give accurate information when you pay or contact us.

3.Your responsibilities
The card is the key. Anyone who holds the activated card, or has its link, can open the greeting. Give the card only to the person it is meant for, and don’t share photos of its QR code or its link.
Check the preview. A greeting can’t be changed after activation.
Keep your editing device. Before payment, editing access is kept on the device you started on, for 14 days. If you change device or clear your browser data, you may not be able to continue.
4.Payment and activation
The price is shown on the card’s start screen and at checkout, before you pay. There is no subscription and no recurring charge.
Your greeting is activated only after the payment is confirmed. If a payment fails, nothing is activated and you can try again.
If you are charged twice for the same greeting, or for a greeting that could not be activated, the extra payment is returned (see Delivery and refunds).
5.Right of withdrawal

Nothing in these terms limits your rights as a consumer under Georgian law.

6.Your content
You are responsible for everything you put in a greeting. You confirm that you have the right to use every photo, video, recording and text you upload, including the permission of the people who appear in them.
You keep ownership of your content. You give us a limited permission to store, process and display it, only to provide the greeting to whoever opens the card.
7.Prohibited content and activities

You must not use QR Starr to create or share content that:

is unlawful, or promotes or incites violence or hatred;
harasses, threatens or defames anyone;
is sexual content involving minors, or otherwise exploits children;
infringes anyone else’s rights, including copyright, trademarks or privacy;
contains malware, or links intended to deceive or cause harm.

You must also not try to access greetings or cards you aren’t entitled to, interfere with the service or its security, or use the service in a way that breaks the law.

9.Service availability

We work to keep greetings available at all times, but we can’t guarantee uninterrupted access. The service may be briefly unavailable for maintenance or because of a fault.

10.Liability

11.Applicable law and disputes
