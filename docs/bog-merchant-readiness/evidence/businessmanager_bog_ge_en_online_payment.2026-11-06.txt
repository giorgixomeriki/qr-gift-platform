Frequently Asked Questions 
What kind of legal person can use Payment methods? The online payments can be used by entities with any legal status (individual entrepreneur, LLC, JSC, etc.) 
What is the needed turnover to use ecommerce? There is no minimum turnover to use ecommerce. 
Can the customers use foreign cards in case of integrating Bank of Georgia’s ecommerce? For using the foreign cards, the features and terms are agreed individually. 
Is having an amount on deposit needed? Yes, depositing amount is needed. The amount should be agreed individually. 
How is the settlement made between bank and the business? The settlement is made on the next calendar day after the transaction. 
Are there any limits for online payments? Yes, the limits should be agreed individually, based on company’s needs.
