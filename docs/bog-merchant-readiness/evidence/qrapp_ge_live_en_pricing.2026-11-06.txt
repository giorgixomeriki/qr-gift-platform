What’s included
Activation of one digital greeting on one QR Starr card.
A written message, up to 3 photos, one video and one voice note, in one of 7 designs.
A preview before you pay.
Opening by scanning the card, as often as the recipient likes.
How and when you pay
You pay once, after previewing your greeting and before it is activated.
The amount is in Georgian lari (GEL) and is shown before you pay.
Nothing is charged if you stop before completing payment.
The price for your card

The standard price applies unless a partner location has a different price agreed with us. The price that applies to your card is the one shown on its start screen and at checkout before you pay, and that is the amount charged.

Price changes

Prices may change over time. A change never affects a greeting you have already paid for: the amount is fixed when you start checkout.

The physical card

The QR Starr card comes with your gift from the partner shop. QR Starr’s fee is the activation fee shown above.

QR Starr

A digital greeting inside every gift: a QR card that opens a personal message, photos, video and voice.
