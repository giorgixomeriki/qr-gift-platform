Delivery, cancellation and refunds

1.What you buy

The activation fee makes your digital greeting available on one QR Starr card. The amount is shown in Georgian lari (GEL) before you pay. See Pricing.

2.Physical QR cards

QR Starr cards are handed out by our partner shops together with the gift you buy from them. QR Starr does not post or ship physical items to customers. Questions about the card itself, or about the gift, go to the shop that provided it.

3.Digital delivery and activation

Delivery is immediate and digital. Your greeting is activated on the card as soon as your payment is confirmed; from then on, scanning the card opens it. Nothing is sent by post.

If a payment fails, nothing is activated and you can try again.

4.Cancelling before payment

You can stop at any time before completing payment. Nothing is charged, and an unpaid greeting is never shown to anyone.

The draft stays reserved on the card and can be continued from the same device for 14 days.

5.After activation

Your greeting is digital content delivered immediately at your request, and it can’t be changed once it is activated.

This policy does not limit your rights as a consumer under Georgian law.

6.When you get a refund

You receive a full refund of the activation fee, to the card you paid with, if:

you were charged but the greeting could not be activated, for example because it had already been paid for, or the card had been paused;
you were charged more than once for the same greeting: the extra payment is returned.
7.How to request a refund
Include the card’s link (the address that opens when you scan the card), the date of payment and the amount.
Tell us briefly what went wrong.
8.How refunds are processed

Refunds are paid back to the card you paid with, through the bank that processed the payment. We refund the full activation fee; partial refunds are not offered. We reply to the email address you wrote from and let you know when the refund has been issued.

9.Card disputes

If you didn’t make a payment yourself, or you want to dispute a payment, please contact us as well as your bank; we can usually resolve it faster. If a dispute (chargeback) is opened, the bank’s decision applies. We may provide evidence that the greeting was delivered, such as when it was activated and opened, but never its content.
