# Celebration — "Signal"

**Emotion:** achievement, pride, energy: a moment worth marking (graduation, promotion, new job, anniversary).

**Avoid:** casino, nightclub, constant confetti, fireworks GIFs, visual chaos, glossy fake gold.

## Composition
An asymmetric poster. A deep cobalt-ink ground; from a point low on the left,
thin marigold lines radiate outwards (a controlled light burst, drawn not
painted). The headline is set big, condensed and left-aligned across the
burst. The message is on a warm-white card tucked low on the right. Three
small four-point sparks, no more.

## Palette
- Ground: midnight ink `#1b2149` → `#0c1030`
- Signal: marigold brass `#f0aa3c`; secondary dusty periwinkle `#8f9ddd`
- Card: warm white `#f6f0e3`; ink `#161a38`
- Type on ground: `#f8f2e6`

## Type
- EN: Instrument Sans 700, wdth 75: condensed, confident
- KA: Noto Sans Georgian 700, wdth 70
- Message: Instrument Sans 400 / Noto Sans Georgian 400

## Material
Flat printed ink: a subtle paper tooth on the ground for a printed, not screen, feel.

## Artwork
A custom SVG burst (about 28 radiating hairlines of varied length) and three Starr-shaped sparks.

## Motion personality
Energetic but controlled. **Signature — "ignition" (WOW pass):** the night is quiet; a Starr ignites at the burst's focal point, the hairlines draw out from it in one sweep across the fan, the opening line and the card arrive, and three sparks complete it. Once, ~1.2s; the focal Starr stays as the composition's anchor.

## Recipient reveal
A controlled burst: the lines expand, the card is struck into place.
