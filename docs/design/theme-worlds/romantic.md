# Romantic — "Quiet light"

**Emotion:** intimate, warm, quiet affection, soft anticipation.

**Avoid:** hearts, petals everywhere, pink overload, Valentine's clipart, wedding stationery.

## Composition
A cream card resting a little off-centre on a dusk-rose ground. Window light
falls across the ground in soft, long rectangles (the room's light, not an
object). A sheet of vellum is tucked across the foot of the card, below the
message (never over it: veiled, the Georgian's second and third lines
washed out on phones). A honeysuckle spray (1775 engraving, Wellcome Collection, CC BY 4.0), printed in the room's light,
leans from the card's edge. Generous dark margin around everything.

## Palette
- Ground: rose clay `#93665e` → oxblood umber `#4d2a2b` at the edges; Poly Haven rough linen
- Light: candle-warm `#ffe3c9` (window light, soft-light)
- Card: aged ivory `#f7eee4`
- Ink: wine-brown `#4a1f26`, soft ink `#85605b`; shadows warm oxblood
- Accent: dried petal `#e6c3b4`

## Type
- EN: EB Garamond Italic 400 (display) / 420 (message) — a letter-writer's italic
- KA: Noto Serif Georgian 340 (Georgian has no italic: warmth comes from the weight and the line spacing)
- Opening line in ground ink (cream on rose); message in wine on the card

## Material
Linen relief (ambientCG Fabric036) soft-lit into the ground; paper tooth on
the card; vellum = translucent cream with paper tooth and a soft edge
shadow (no blur glass).

## Artwork
The honeysuckle engraving as a single-colour mask (`public/customer/engravings/`), its lower stem hidden behind the card. Replaced the hand-drawn SVG stem in the professional pass.
That is all.

## Motion personality
Soft, slow, warm. **Signature — "light finds the letter" (WOW pass):** the room starts at dusk; the window light travels across the linen and comes to rest; the honeysuckle appears where it lands; the card is set down (spring settle, no overshoot) and the vellum tucked under it. ~1.8s full, then still.

## Recipient reveal (for the reveal screen)
The vellum lifts away from the card, slowly, and the message is read clear.
