# Birthday — "Paper party"

**Emotion:** joy, energy, personality, surprise, warm celebration.

**Avoid:** children's clipart, rainbow, balloons, emoji, confetti explosions.

## Composition
A poster made of cut paper. Persimmon ground; a large saffron paper disc rises
behind the headline like a low sun; a pink cut arc and a deep-teal cut
streamer (one wavy strip) overlap the edges. Each paper layer has its own
crisp shadow, so the stack reads as physical. The headline is set big and
heavy on the ground; the message sits on a cream paper slip pinned at a slight
angle.

## Palette
- Ground: persimmon `#d85a31` (a little less chroma)
- Cut paper: saffron `#edb046`, dusty peony `#eaa4ac`, bottle teal `#1e5f5b`, cream `#fcf2e3`; warm-brown shadows
- Ink: plum-black `#2b1327`

## Type
- EN: Fraunces 800, SOFT 100, WONK 1, opsz 96: heavy, soft, playful
- KA: Noto Serif Georgian 800: a serif like Fraunces, the same weight and spirit
- Message: Fraunces 560 / Noto Serif Georgian 600

## Material
Matte coloured paper: the paper tooth tinted per layer; hard little shadows (no blur glow).

## Artwork
Custom SVG cut-paper shapes only: a disc, an arc, a wavy streamer, three
small punched dots. No candles, cake or balloons.

## Motion personality
Quick, joyful, physical — the fastest world. **Signature — "cut paper snaps in" (WOW pass):** each piece arrives on its own path and springs into place (snap spring, 10% overshoot): the disc rolls in, the arc swings up, the streamer unfurls, the card is tossed down, the dots land. Settled by ~0.5s; never bounces again.

## Recipient reveal
The layers pop open one after another in a tasteful physical rhythm; the slip
lands last.
