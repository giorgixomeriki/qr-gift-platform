# Elegant — "Ink & stone"

**Emotion:** luxury, confidence, taste, restraint, timeless editorial quality.

**Avoid:** black plus fake gold, gloss, marble, luxury-car website, over-decoration.

## Composition
Architectural. A deep warm-ink ground with a linen weave in it. A tall,
narrow stone-coloured card stands off-centre with a long, soft shadow. On the
card a fine inset hairline frame, the opening line in a Didone, a thin rule
and the message. A single vertical hairline runs down the ground beside the
card. Vast negative space.

## Palette
- Ground: warm graphite `#282421` → `#181614`, Poly Haven rough linen
- Card: bone `#e3dbcf`
- Ink: `#231f1b`; soft ink `#5f5850`
- Rules: stone at 35% on the ground; ink at 40% on the card
- **No gold.**

## Type
- EN: Gilda Display 400 (display and message) — Didone contrast without the chill
- KA: Noto Serif Georgian 250 (display) / 300 (message), normal width

## Material
Linen ground (Fabric036 relief, low contrast); stone card stock (paper tooth); no shine.

## Artwork
Hairline rules only, plus a small, centred Starr blind mark at the card's foot.

## Motion personality
Restrained, editorial. **Signature — "aperture" (WOW pass):** the fine rule is drawn down the graphite; the bone card opens from its own centre line like a spread; the inner frame settles onto it; the words are inked in last. ~1.4s full, then still.

## Recipient reveal
A typographic composition assembling: rule, title, message, signature.
