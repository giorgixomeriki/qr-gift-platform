# Wedding — "Vellum & emboss"

**Emotion:** ceremony, timelessness, commitment, quiet importance.

**Avoid:** flower arches, gold script, stock roses, Pinterest templates, making QR Starr look wedding-specific.

## Composition
Formal and symmetrical. An ivory card centred on a pale ivory-grey ground,
framed by a double hairline rule. At the top corner, an olive spray (W. H. Fitch, 1874, public domain) is printed pale and
blind-embossed (no ink, relief only). The opening line is centred in a formal
Garamond; below it a hairline and the message. A vellum band crosses the
upper third, so the olive shows softly through it. Lots of whitespace.

## Palette
- Ground: sage stone `#b4b6a4` → `#9c9f8b` (the card glows against it)
- Card: cotton white `#fbf9f3`
- Ink: olive-black `#2b332c`, soft ink `#5d695e`; olive-grey shadows
- Olive print and Starr: `#76845f`; rule `#2b332c` at 30%

## Type
- EN: EB Garamond 400 (display), EB Garamond Italic 400 (message)
- KA: Noto Serif Georgian 400 (display) / 300 (message); no tracking on Georgian

## Material
Cotton card (paper tooth), vellum band (translucent ivory with tooth), blind
emboss using the same lit-edge filter as the QR Starr maker's mark.

## Artwork
Fitch's olive engraving printed letterpress-pale with a faint impression; a double rule frame; a centred
three-point divider.

## Motion personality
Slow, ceremonial, precise. **Signature — "the vellum is drawn off" (WOW pass):** the card first appears under a full vellum sheet, its words misty; after a held beat (~330ms) the sheet is drawn upward and away, lifting toward the viewer, and the letterpress comes up crisp; the belly band is tucked back last. One dominant moment; ~1.5s full, then still.

## Recipient reveal
The vellum is drawn back like folded stationery opened by hand.
